Where development is now

v0.20.x development line

Core Sorcerer progression, reserves, and compatibility

The current development line expands the long-term growth of Sorcerers while preserving the combat foundation already available in the public alpha.

  • Potential, Learning Rate, Tier Mastery, and automatic normal-tier progression are implemented in development.
  • Physical Enhancement has been rebalanced and Max Cursed Energy now grows with Sorcerer development while retaining individual variation.
  • Old-save migration has been hardened so existing Sorcerers keep stable reserve state and a single authoritative Sorcerer Tier.
  • v0.20.4 adds compatibility work so Cursed Barrage can participate correctly in relevant Legends unarmed-perk behavior.

Important: v0.20.4 is development source, not the current downloadable public package. The published alpha remains v0.19.0 until a newer build completes testing and release preparation.

Established foundation

Available

Sorcerer identity & Cursed Energy

Sorcerer traits, tiers, Cursed Energy reserves, campaign and tactical CE displays, and the dedicated Sorcery interface.

Available

Mastery & Physical Enhancement

Cursed Output, Cursed Resilience, CE Refinement, and passive physical growth tied to practical jujutsu development.

Available

Experiential technique learning

Pressure, technique-specific Insight, natural breakthroughs, and combat-driven acquisition rather than a conventional perk-only progression path.

Available

Common CE Applications

Cursed Reinforcement, Cursed Infusion, Cursed Strike, Cursed Barrage, Burst Step, and Focused Impact form the current common-technique foundation.

Available

Negative Surge

An involuntary survival phenomenon that can reveal cursed capability under extreme pressure and contributes to a Sorcerer's personal progression.

Published

v0.19.0 First Public Alpha

The first public package established the playable Sorcerer-focused alpha and remains the current downloadable release while v0.20.x is tested.
