Where JJKCE is now

Current public release · v0.21.12

Sorcerer Progression Update

v0.21.12 is now the current public release, expanding the original Sorcerer alpha with deeper progression, progressive CE Reserves, natural awakening, ordinary-campaign Sorcerer recruitment, compatibility work, and extensive stabilization.

  • Develop Potential, Learning Rate, Tier Mastery, Advancement Credit, Cursed Output, Cursed Resilience, CE Refinement, and Physical Enhancement through play.
  • Grow individual Cursed Energy Reserves as a Sorcerer develops, with permanent personal reserve variation and Potential influence.
  • Ordinary brothers can naturally awaken through several dangerous combat and campaign situations.
  • Rare ordinary settlement recruits can already be Sorcerers, allowing jujutsu to emerge in campaigns that do not begin with The Newly Awakened origin.
  • Six Common CE Applications, Pressure, Insight, Negative Surge, experiential learning, campaign/tactical CE UI, and the dedicated Sorcery interface remain the core playable foundation.

Development continues after v0.21.12: immediate work returns to post-release maintenance while the next major roadmap phase begins building the cursed-damage and supernatural-world framework.

Established public foundation

Available

Sorcerer identity & Cursed Energy

Sorcerer traits, tiers, individual Cursed Energy, progressive reserves, campaign and tactical CE displays, and the dedicated Sorcery interface.

Available

Mastery, progression & Physical Enhancement

Cursed Output, Cursed Resilience, CE Refinement, Potential, Learning Rate, Tier Mastery, Advancement Credit, and passive physical development form the current Sorcerer progression model.

Available

Experiential technique learning

Pressure, technique-specific Insight, natural breakthroughs, and combat-driven acquisition rather than a conventional perk-only progression path.

Available

Common CE Applications

Cursed Reinforcement, Cursed Infusion, Cursed Strike, Cursed Barrage, Burst Step, and Focused Impact form the current common-technique foundation.

Available

Negative Surge

An involuntary survival phenomenon that can reveal cursed capability under extreme pressure and contributes to a Sorcerer's personal progression.

Available

Campaign Sorcerers

The Newly Awakened origin remains the dedicated Sorcerer start, while natural awakening and rare generated Sorcerer recruits now allow jujutsu to emerge in ordinary campaigns.

Now available in v0.21.12

Available

Deeper Sorcerer progression

Potential, Learning Rate, Tier Mastery, automatic normal Tier advancement, Advancement Credit, and smoother long-term mastery growth expand the original progression foundation.

Available

Progressive CE Reserves & Physical Enhancement

Max CE develops with the Sorcerer, including individual reserve variation and Potential influence, while Physical Enhancement uses a more deliberate physical/kinetic defensive balance.

Available

Natural Awakening

Ordinary brothers can manifest as Sorcerers through several dangerous combat and campaign situations, including lethal survival, protection, vengeance, last-stand, desperate-victory, guided, and breaking-point paths.

Available

Sorcerer recruits in ordinary campaigns

Rare ordinary settlement recruits can already be Sorcerers, with company progression influencing how experienced those generated Sorcerers may be. Ordinary recruitment never produces a starting Master Sorcerer.

Available

Optional Legends compatibility improvements

Cursed Barrage has targeted optional support for relevant Legends unarmed mechanics while JJKCE remains usable without Legends as a hard dependency.

Published

v0.21.12 stabilization

The release also includes save-state, tier-integrity, Modern Hooks, generated-recruit, skill-container, UI, artwork, and compatibility stabilization accumulated since the first public alpha.
