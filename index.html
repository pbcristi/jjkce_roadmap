Where JJKCE is now

Current public release · v0.22.1

Technical Hotfix & Cursed Damage Foundation

v0.22.1 is the current downloadable public release. It retains the established Sorcerer progression systems while adding technical stabilization and the combat foundation required by future curse enemies.

  • Generated Sorcerer Tier initialization has been stabilized, including a narrow repair path for missing persisted generated tiers.
  • The tactical character panel/hotbar refresh path has been hardened against stale delayed updates.
  • The Newly Awakened origin has targeted Legends starting-body-armor compatibility while preserving the vanilla fallback.
  • The Cursed Damage Framework now distinguishes ordinary physical attacks from CE-infused and cursed-technique damage for future supernatural targets.
  • Development/debug menus are disabled in the public configuration.
Published foundation · v0.22

Cursed Damage Framework

The curse-valid damage layer is now part of the public mod. It keeps ordinary Battle Brothers targets on the vanilla damage path while providing explicit semantics for future supernatural targets.

  • Ordinary physical damage is distinct from CE-infused and cursed-technique damage.
  • Cursed Infusion validates its qualifying direct attack rather than creating an extra damage packet.
  • Cursed Strike, Cursed Barrage, and attacks under Focused Impact have explicit cursed-technique semantics.
  • Actual pure curse enemies and the full curse-perception system are not included yet.

Current state: v0.22.1 is published. The next major milestone is the first Basic Curse Ecosystem.

Compatibility

The Newly Awakened origin now uses Legends' normal layered body-armor construction when available while retaining the original vanilla fallback without Legends.
