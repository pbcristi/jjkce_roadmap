Where development is now

v0.20.1 · Current source

Physical Enhancement Balance & CE Reserves

The v0.20 core progression foundation remains implemented and awaiting runtime/save-load verification. The current v0.20.1 follow-up adds the accepted Physical Enhancement defensive rebalance and derived CE Reserves model with compatibility-oriented save migration.

Implemented / verification pending
  • Physical Enhancement keeps its established offense, skill, Initiative, and AP architecture while using the accepted 30% / 20% / 20% / 50% / 50% intrinsic defensive coefficients.
  • Intrinsic PE mitigation is physical/kinetic only; poison, DamageOverTime, and explicitly nonphysical or cursed sources do not gain generic PE protection.
  • Developed CE Reserves scale from about 100 CE at Tier Mastery 0 to 5000 CE at Tier Mastery 500 before innate and Potential variation.
  • Each new Sorcerer receives one permanent weighted innate reserve factor centered toward 1.00, while existing Sorcerers use a one-time compatibility migration intended to preserve their prior reserve size.
  • Potential modifies reserve size, final Max CE is capped at 10000, and increasing Max CE does not refill established Current CE.
  • Post-Master reserve growth remains deliberately deferred; the next major roadmap objective remains v0.21.0 after v0.20.x verification.

The public Nexus/Discord package remains v0.19.0 until a later package is built, verified, and uploaded. Source version and public release version are intentionally tracked separately.
