Where JJKCE is now

Current public release

First Public Alpha

The downloadable alpha establishes the playable Sorcerer foundation. New progression, reserve, awakening, and recruitment systems already exist in development and are being stabilized for a future public update.

  • Create and play Sorcerers with dedicated Cursed Energy, tiers, character/tactical CE displays, and a dedicated Sorcery interface.
  • Develop Cursed Output, Cursed Resilience, CE Refinement, Physical Enhancement, Pressure, and technique-specific Insight through play.
  • Discover and learn the current Common CE Applications through experiential progression rather than a conventional perk-only path.
  • Experience The Newly Awakened origin and the unstable Negative Surge survival phenomenon.

Development continues: newer work is shown below by feature or broad milestone only. Exact internal source/build versions are intentionally not displayed on this public page.

Established public foundation

Available

Sorcerer identity & Cursed Energy

Sorcerer traits, tiers, individual Cursed Energy, campaign and tactical CE displays, and the dedicated Sorcery interface.

Available

Mastery & Physical Enhancement

Cursed Output, Cursed Resilience, CE Refinement, and passive physical growth tied to practical jujutsu development.

Available

Experiential technique learning

Pressure, technique-specific Insight, natural breakthroughs, and combat-driven acquisition rather than a conventional perk-only progression path.

Available

Common CE Applications

Cursed Reinforcement, Cursed Infusion, Cursed Strike, Cursed Barrage, Burst Step, and Focused Impact form the current common-technique foundation.

Available

Negative Surge

An involuntary survival phenomenon that can reveal cursed capability under extreme pressure and contributes to a Sorcerer's personal progression.

Published

The Newly Awakened

The dedicated Sorcerer origin and Awakened Sorcerer background provide the current public campaign entry point into JJKCE.

Implemented in development

Implemented

Deeper Sorcerer progression

Potential, Learning Rate, Tier Mastery, automatic normal Tier advancement, Advancement Credit, and smoother long-term mastery growth expand the original progression foundation.

Implemented

Progressive CE Reserves & Physical Enhancement

Max CE now develops with the Sorcerer, including individual reserve variation and Potential influence, while Physical Enhancement has received a more deliberate physical/kinetic defensive balance.

Implemented

Natural Awakening

Ordinary brothers can manifest as Sorcerers through several dangerous combat and campaign situations, including lethal survival, protection, vengeance, last-stand, desperate-victory, guided, and breaking-point paths.

Implemented

Sorcerer recruits in ordinary campaigns

Rare ordinary settlement recruits can already be Sorcerers, with company progression influencing how experienced those generated Sorcerers may be. Ordinary recruitment never produces a starting Master Sorcerer.

Implemented

Optional Legends compatibility improvements

Cursed Barrage has targeted optional support for relevant Legends unarmed mechanics while JJKCE remains usable without Legends as a hard dependency.

Current verification

Release-candidate stabilization

Current work is focused on clean startup, hidden development UI, origin and ordinary-campaign smoke tests, combat behavior, save/reload persistence, and supported no-Legends/Legends compatibility before the next public update.
